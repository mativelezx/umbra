# Workflow: Business Decision

1. Classify the input with `SOLO_FOUNDER_CONTROL_PLANE.md`.
2. Gather current context: live product/code evidence, active decisions, beta scope, metrics/feedback, docs as hypotheses, accepted debt, and approval gates.
3. Gather business context from `docs/biz/` and mark stale or unverified claims.
4. Use `.agent-os/BUSINESS_GTM_CONTRACT.md` when the decision affects ICP, pricing, packaging, activation, retention, GTM, marketing claims, channel strategy, support model, cost/margin, or founder/investor/CTO business claims.
5. Use `.agent-os/PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_CONTRACT.md` when the decision affects category language, positioning, naming, claims, onboarding/landing copy, or AI trust language.
6. Use `.agent-os/FOUNDER_COMMUNICATION_CONTRACT.md` when the decision affects founder story, social channels, public launch, content pillars, proof, audience/ICP bridge, founder-led sales, investor/CTO narrative, or public founder trust.
7. Research market/current external context when claims, vendors, pricing, GTM, communication channels, reference founders, compliance, or platform behavior can change.
8. Run premortem for high-cost decisions.
9. Compare options with user value, revenue, cost, risk, reversibility, founder workload, legal/compliance impact, continuity/recovery impact, vendor/IP impact, founder communication impact, and CTO handoff impact.
10. Ask founder approval for durable or risky decisions.
11. Record decision in `DECISION_SYNC_CONTRACT.md` format and update existing docs only after implementation/verification when applicable.
